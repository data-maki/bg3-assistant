# Windows MVP scope amendment - 2026-07-25

The project owner approved these Windows MVP exclusions:

- live BG3 installation, renderer, gameplay, and input verification;
- screenshot capture, image attachments, clipboard-image ingestion, and their permissions;
- microphone access, speech recognition, and dictation.

G0 uses controlled x64 executables/windows named `bg3.exe` and `bg3_dx11.exe` to verify
the documented process/window contract, movement, resize, minimize/close, foreground
changes, negative coordinates, Windowed/Borderless-style geometry, and display/DPI
behavior. Evidence must call these controlled hosts and must not imply live BG3 coverage.

Parity rows A-07, A-08, and A-09 are approved MVP exclusions. The capture and
microphone portions of I-13 are also excluded; its remaining startup, Credential Manager,
and outbound-HTTPS permission boundaries still require evidence.

The shipped application must contain no capture/speech product UI, service, package
capability, or runtime dependency. Earlier capture and microphone experiments are
historical development evidence only and do not satisfy or block the revised MVP.
